# LocalClear privacy notice

Status: **Draft for product and engineering review — not legal advice and not approved for publication.** Privacy/Legal must supply the operating entity, jurisdiction-specific disclosures, contact details, vendor list, international-transfer terms, and effective date.

LocalClear helps a user photograph household items, prepare local listings, coordinate buyer tasks, and record clearing outcomes. It processes account identity, household preferences and approximate ZIP/radius, item photos and descriptions, listing and publishing state, limited redacted buyer-message excerpts, meetup choices, device health, and sale outcomes to provide those functions.

LocalClear does not connect a payment account or process marketplace payments. Marketplace login credentials, cookies, session tokens, and MFA codes stay inside the marketplace’s official app on the user’s Seller Hub and must never be uploaded to LocalClear.

Photos are stored privately. Location EXIF is removed before external publishing. Likely faces, documents, addresses, and license plates are flagged for user review. Exact home addresses are not inserted into suggested messages or listings without explicit approval. Support diagnostics are optional, separately consented, redacted, privacy-scanned, scoped to an assigned operator, access-audited, and time-limited.

Approved service providers may process authentication, private storage/database, AI enrichment, push delivery, and telemetry under written data-protection terms. AI and pricing adapters receive only the data needed for the requested task. LocalClear does not sell personal information or use marketplace credentials for advertising.

Users can access/export their data, correct editable records, revoke a Seller Hub, revoke support access, and delete an item or account. A final notice must explain verified request channels, statutory exceptions, appeal rights, state/country rights, children’s eligibility, retention periods, security practices, changes, and regulator/contact information.
